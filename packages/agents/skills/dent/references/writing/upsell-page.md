# Write an upsell Funnel Step

Use this after `copywriting.md` has clarified the shared promise, proof, and Offer from `interview.md`. The failure it prevents: treating an Upsell Funnel Step like a second sales page, re-opening payment doubt, or making the buyer regret Checkout.

## Process

1. Confirm the post-Checkout context.

   - Consume `surface`, `page_job`, `audience`, `customer_situation`, `customer_language`, `promise`, `offer`, `differentiation`, `proof_points`, `proof_gaps`, `objections`, `reader_awareness_stage`, `market_sophistication`, `register`, `mood`, `visual_direction`, `constraints`, and `open_questions` from the brief.
   - Treat the reader as most-aware because they just bought or accepted the main Checkout path.
   - Confirm the original purchase first, then present one relevant upgrade.
   - Use the one-click accept path required by `constraints`; do not ask the Contact to re-enter payment details.

   IF the upgrade is not clearly related to what they just bought:
   ### Stop and ask for a closer Offer
   An upsell works because it extends the purchase momentum, not because the buyer is trapped.
   Never: pitch an unrelated Course, Space, or Product because the Contact already paid.

2. Use the upsell block order.

   - Confirmation line: acknowledge the purchase or enrollment is complete.
   - Upgrade hero: name the exclusive now-only add-on and the better outcome it unlocks.
   - Complement line: explain why this Offer belongs with the original purchase.
   - One proof point: use the strongest relevant proof, demo, inclusion, or credibility marker.
   - Why now: state the real timing reason for one-click acceptance.
   - Dominant yes: one button that adds the upgrade to the existing Order or Checkout flow.
   - Graceful no: one plain decline Link that preserves trust and continues the Funnel.

   ### Keep the page short
   The buyer has already made the hard decision.
   Never: rebuild the full sales argument, restate every feature, or add a multi-step comparison table.

3. Write the headline around the upgrade, not fear.

   - Lead with the extra outcome the buyer can add now.
   - Tie the upgrade to the main Offer using the buyer's current momentum.
   - Use `customer_language` only where it reinforces the next win.

   ### Make the yes feel like a natural next step
   The copy should say "this helps what you just bought work better".
   Example: "Add the implementation sprint to finish your first Funnel this week".
   Never: "Wait — your order is incomplete".

4. Prove relevance fast.

   - Use one `proof_points` item that supports the upgrade's specific value.
   - Lower or omit claims listed in `proof_gaps`.
   - Answer objections about price, duplicate value, overwhelm, access, support, and whether they can decide later.

   IF the urgency is only that the Funnel Step appears now:
   ### Name access timing, not false scarcity
   One-click convenience is a real now-reason when the system can add the Offer immediately.
   Never: invent a deadline or pretend the Offer disappears unless that is true.

5. Write yes and no microcopy cleanly.

   - Make the accept CTA explicit, outcome-oriented, and one-click.
   - Keep the decline visible, calm, and free of guilt.
   - Tell the buyer what happens after each choice when the next step could be unclear.

   ### Never shame the decline
   Trust after the purchase is worth more than a forced click.
   Example: "Yes, add the sprint to my Order" and "No thanks, continue to my access".
   Never: "No, I do not want faster results".

6. Route drafted copy through the final editing pass.

   AI tells, pompous wording, and wall-of-text cleanup before design → `writing/editing.md`
