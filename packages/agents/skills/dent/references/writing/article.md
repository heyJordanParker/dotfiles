# Write an Article

Use this after `copywriting.md` has separated the Article's reader promise from any sales pressure in the brief from `interview.md`. The failure it prevents: turning useful content into a thin sales Page, or publishing an Article with no earned next step.

## Process

1. Confirm the Article promise.

   - Consume `surface`, `page_job`, `audience`, `customer_situation`, `customer_language`, `promise`, `offer`, `differentiation`, `proof_points`, `proof_gaps`, `objections`, `reader_awareness_stage`, `market_sophistication`, `register`, `mood`, `visual_direction`, `constraints`, and `open_questions` from the brief.
   - Treat the typical reader as unaware, problem-aware, or solution-aware unless the source says they are warmer.
   - Make the Article satisfy a reading job before it asks for action.
   - Keep the CTA contextual to the Article, not a hard-sell detour.

   IF `page_job` is to close a paid Offer:
   ### Use the sales page leaf instead
   An Article can assist a sale, but its first obligation is the reader promise.
   Never: disguise a sales Page as education.

2. Use the Article block order.

   - Headline: earn the read with the specific problem, promise, angle, or useful curiosity.
   - Deck or intro: name who it is for, why it matters now, and what the reader will understand or do by the end.
   - Orientation: define the situation using `customer_language`, not internal jargon.
   - Main sections: teach the argument in a clear sequence with useful subheads.
   - Examples or proof: use `proof_points`, screenshots, stories, data, product examples, or operator experience where they clarify the lesson.
   - Practical takeaway: summarize what the reader should do or believe next.
   - One contextual CTA: lead magnet, related Page, Checkout, Course, Space, or next Article only when it naturally follows the content.

   ### Make the headings carry the lesson
   A scanner should understand the Article's argument from the section headings alone.
   Never: use vague headings like "Introduction", "Overview", or "Conclusion" when a useful claim can name the section.

3. Write the headline for attention and accuracy.

   - Use specificity from `audience`, `customer_situation`, or `promise`.
   - Make curiosity honest; the Article must pay off the headline.
   - Avoid inflated certainty when `proof_gaps` limit the claim.

   ### Earn the click without breaking trust
   The headline should make the right reader feel seen and then deliver.
   Example: "Why your lead magnet is collecting emails but not starting sales conversations".
   Never: "The secret every creator must know".

4. Teach before selling.

   - Use `differentiation` to frame the named alternative only when it clarifies the lesson.
   - Put proof where it helps the reader believe or apply the point.
   - Answer reader objections about relevance, effort, prerequisites, risk, and what changes after they act.
   - Keep Dent entity names precise when the Article references Funnel Steps, Offers, Checkout, Contacts, Courses, Spaces, Pages, or Links.

   IF a Product or Offer appears in the body:
   ### Make it an example until the CTA
   The Article can show the Offer as the natural next step after the lesson is complete.
   Never: interrupt every section with a purchase pitch.

5. Write one contextual CTA.

   - Pick the next action that follows the Article's promise and awareness stage.
   - Use opt-in CTAs for colder readers, sales or Checkout CTAs only for warmer readers, and related Articles when the reader needs more education.
   - Make the CTA copy name the next useful outcome.
   - Keep supporting microcopy clear about what happens after the click.

   ### Let the content determine the ask
   The CTA should feel like the next helpful step, not an ad bolted to the bottom.
   Example: "Get the Funnel Step checklist".
   Never: "Buy the Offer now" after an early-stage educational Article with no buying argument.

6. Route drafted copy through the final editing pass.

   AI tells, pompous wording, and wall-of-text cleanup before design → `writing/editing.md`
