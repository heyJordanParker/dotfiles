# Google / search ad

Earn the click from someone already searching, on the exact keyword, and set the expectation the landing page will continue.

- **Job:** win the click from active intent and hand it to a page that continues the query.
- **Stage:** problem-aware to product-aware. They typed the query, so meet it on its own terms.
- **Recipe:** headline on the searched query → one line of value or proof → CTA. Match the structure to awareness — a problem hook or PAS for the broad query, an offer for the high-intent one.
- **Moves:**
  - Lead with the angle that matches the keyword, not the brand — the hook carries ~80% of performance.
  - Whatever the ad promises, the landing page must open on the same words and continue the exact query (message match).
- **Mistake:** an ad that wins the click on a promise the page does not keep.
