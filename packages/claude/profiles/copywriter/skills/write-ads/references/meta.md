# Meta / social feed ad

Interrupt a feed and earn the click from someone who was not looking — the creative is the targeting.

- **Job:** earn the click from a cold reader mid-scroll. They did not ask, so the creative has to create the want.
- **Stage:** unaware to problem-aware. Lead with the problem, not the offer (a problem hook or PAS).
- **Recipe:** native, user-generated-style creative → a thumb-stopping hook in the first line or first ~3 seconds → one line of value or proof → CTA.
- **Moves:**
  - The ad must not look like an ad — native, user-generated-style creative outperforms polished brand creative, which the feed trains readers to skip.
  - The hook in the first line or first ~3 seconds carries ~80% of performance.
  - The page still must keep the promise (message match) — a thumb-stopping click the page then fails to continue is wasted.
- **Mistake:** a creative that reads as an ad and gets skipped on sight, or a click the landing page does not continue.
