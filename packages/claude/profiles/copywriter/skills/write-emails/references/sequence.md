# Sequence

A lifecycle series triggered by where the user is and what they did. Sequences compound — each email does one job and hands to the next. Trigger on behavior (hit a limit, used a premium feature, a win landed) over time wherever you can.

- **Onboarding** — 5-7 emails over ~14 days, each driving one step toward the first win: welcome + single next step → quick win → key feature → success story → check-in → advanced tip → upgrade prompt. Goal: activation. Mistake: dumping every feature in email one.
- **New customer** — 3-5 emails reinforcing the purchase and driving adoption. They committed; reassure and expand, do not re-sell.
- **Step reminder** — one email or a short series when a high-value setup step stalls: remind what they started, say why it matters, link straight to finishing.
- **Trial-ending / upgrade** — value summary (what they accomplished) → what they are missing → social proof → the deadline → last chance. Trigger on behavior over time where you can.
- **Failed payment** — 3-4 emails, no guilt, assume an expired card. One CTA: update payment. Escalate clarity on what they lose, not the pressure.
- **Usage report** — recurring; show what they accomplished and the value delivered. Never send an empty report — it reminds them they do not use it.
- **Win-back (expired trial / churned)** — what is new since they left → the reason they left, addressed → an invitation to return. No desperation; they return when their original reason is fixed.
- **Referral / expansion (happy, activated user)** — trigger on a success signal. One ask, never both at once: refer someone who would get the same result, or expand (seats, plan, usage). Lead with the value they already got. Mistake: asking before the user has felt a win.

Across the series: one job per email, one CTA per email; each email assumes the same user the last one left, and answers the question that email raised.
