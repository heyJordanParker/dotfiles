# Launch

A launch email series concentrates attention and a reason to act now into a short window. The series runs to a real deadline; a fake one, caught once, costs every future launch.

- **Job:** carry a warm audience (list, community) through a launch window to one action, across a short series of sends.
- **Stage:** your warmest audience first — often most-aware by the close of the window.
- **Shape (typical window series):** announce (the news is the hook) → the offer and what it does → proof and story → objection-handling → the deadline approaching → last call. Each send does one job and raises the reason to act now.
- **Moves:**
  - The news is the hook on the opening send; pair it with a genuine why-now (a founding price that truly ends, an early-access cap that is real).
  - Escalate urgency across the window on the real deadline only — never manufacture one.
  - Each send stands alone for the reader who missed the last, and still moves the window forward.
- **Mistake:** manufactured scarcity. The internet is small; you will be caught, and it costs every launch after.
