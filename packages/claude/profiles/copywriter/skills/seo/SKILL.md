# SEO

What this advisor supplies: the search reality the strategy call needs — which demand is real and reachable, what searchers at each stage actually want, and what it honestly takes to be found, in both classic search and AI-generated answers. The job is a feasibility read, not a twelve-month plan.

## Demand and intent by buyer stage

Search demand maps to where the buyer is. The modifiers reveal the stage, and the stage dictates the intent the page must satisfy:

- **Awareness** — "what is," "how to," "guide to." They want to understand the problem, not buy. Educational content matches.
- **Consideration** — "best," "top," "vs," "alternatives." They're comparing. Comparison and alternative pages match.
- **Decision** — "pricing," "reviews," "demo," "trial." They're close. Product, pricing, and proof pages match.
- **Implementation** — "templates," "examples," "tutorial," "setup." Already a user or already committed. Practical how-to matches.

This search funnel (Awareness/Consideration/Decision/Implementation) is the buyer's search journey — distinct from the reader-awareness model the copywriting skill owns. Keep them separate when handing the chief a read.

Match the page to the intent exactly. A sales page ranked for a "how to" query converts no one and, because it doesn't satisfy the searcher, ranks for nothing.

## Ranking reality

The honest read for a solo founder, not the trick list:

- **You rank by deserving to.** Search rewards content that satisfies the intent better than what's already there — real depth, first-hand experience, original information. No on-page trick beats deserving the spot.
- **Foundations are table stakes.** The page has to be crawlable, indexable, fast, and sound on mobile, or nothing above matters. These are pass/fail, not optimizations.
- **SEO is a 6–12 month marathon.** It compounds for years once it works and pays nothing for months. For a solo founder it's a long-game bet, not a launch channel.
- **Pick winnable demand.** Against an entrenched competitor you won't rank for the head term early. Win the specific, lower-competition long-tail where intent is high and the giants are thin — `[problem] for [specific role]` — and build topical authority outward from there.

## AI search reality

More search now resolves inside AI answers — AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity — and the rules differ:

- **Classic search gets you ranked; AI search gets you cited.** A well-structured page can be cited even when it ranks on page two, because AI selects on quality and structure, not position alone.
- **Structure for extraction.** Lead each section with a direct answer; keep the key answer tight enough to stand on its own; use headings that match how people phrase the question; use a table for a comparison. AI extracts passages, not whole pages.
- **Authority earns citations.** Cite sources, include specific statistics with dates, attribute claims to named experts. The largest measured citation boosts come from citing sources and adding statistics; keyword stuffing actively hurts.
- **Presence beyond your own site.** AI cites where you appear — Reddit, Wikipedia, review sites — often more than your own pages. Authentic third-party presence compounds.
- **Write for people, organize for clarity.** That one default satisfies both Google, which treats AI-only content as spam, and the other engines. Don't write a separate version for the machines.

## What good SEO judgment hands over

A feasibility read: which search demand is real and reachable at the product's stage, what intent the copy has to match by buyer stage, the on-page essentials, and where AI-search citation is worth pursuing — so the chief bets on search demand that can actually be won.
