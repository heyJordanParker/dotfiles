# Editing Checklist

The whole ship gate in tick form. Each editor runs its own group; the chief running a small ask inline runs all four. The definition and the why for each check live in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) — this is the run-down to confirm none was skipped before returning. Copy clears only when every check in scope passes.

## Line — reads clean

- [ ] **L1 Clarity** — every sentence understood on one read; pronouns resolve; no shared-jargon gaps
- [ ] **L2 Active voice** — verb carries the sentence; no passive where active works; no buried nominalization; no expletive opener (*there is/are/were*); no chained passive
- [ ] **L3 Word economy** — no filler intensifier, dead connective, or removable *that*; thin sentences carrying one idea fused into one
- [ ] **L4 Plain words** — no pompous word with a plain twin; no empty abstraction noun (*case, character, nature, system, factor, feature*) for the concrete thing (see plain-english.md)
- [ ] **L5 Rhythm** — reads aloud clean; length varies; no slack *and/but/who/which/while* clause-chain; key word front-loaded (deliberate periodic sentence or emphasis fragment excepted)
- [ ] **L6 Positive form** — says what a thing is, not what it isn't; no needless negative with a positive twin
- [ ] **L7 Related words together** — every modifier next to what it modifies; no meaning-bending misplacement

## Voice — sounds like this product

- [ ] **V1 Voice match** — matches `Voice.md` is/is-not, lexicon, register (provisional voice → flag)
- [ ] **V2 One voice** — consistent end to end; no shift in formality, person, or tone
- [ ] **V3 Terminology** — uses the terms `Voice.md` keeps; avoids the ones it bans

## Slop — nothing generic

- [ ] **S1 No AI tells** — none from ai-tells.md
- [ ] **S2 No em-dash crutch** — at most one deliberate em dash per screen
- [ ] **S3 No SaaS slop** — no generic superlative; no unbacked *best/leading*
- [ ] **S4 No AI structure** — no *whether-you're / it's-not-just / By-[verb-ing]* shapes
- [ ] **S5 Distinctiveness** (additive) — no line a competitor could paste unchanged; nothing forgettable

## Strategy — earns the next step

- [ ] **ST1 Awareness match** — pitched to where the reader is; sophistication fits the market
- [ ] **ST2 So what** — every claim answers "why should I care?"; outcome leads, mechanism follows
- [ ] **ST3 Specific** — number/timeframe/named outcome over vague adjective
- [ ] **ST4 Proof** — every promise backed; no unearned superlative; no invented proof
- [ ] **ST5 One job, clear ask** — one action, one specific CTA; objections at the CTA reversed
- [ ] **ST6 Emotion** — the reader feels the pain or want, not just reads it
