# Research Market

Run /research; this adds only the market axis. research owns the trust check, the page gate, the fetched-page record rule, the listicle and SEO-blog ban, the verbatim-or-PARAPHRASE record format with its URL and dates, and the write to `research/<subject>/<topic>.md` — hold to it and add only what follows.

Inputs: the one part of the market's structure, from discover-market's lists, and the domain from the research `Brief.md` — nothing else. Excluded: owner facts, product facts, any persona or target buyer, and any solution shape — you mine what the market shows about itself, never confirm a guess. The dispatch carries this part and the domain only, with no reference to a sibling thread.

## Mine the one part of the market deep

Take the one part discovery surfaced and mine it deep from primary surfaces — the sellers' own pricing and buying pages, the platforms and marketplaces that carry the category, industry bodies and public filings — capturing verbatim, each sorted into its own topic file:

- **How money moves** — the exact pricing and billing shapes the sellers publish: the tiers, the price points, the billing model, quoted from the page they sit on.
- **How people buy** — the observed buying path the category shows: the signup flow, the sales motion, the marketplace or agency route, described from what the page shows.
- **Sizes and spend** — the countable figures the market publishes about itself: revenue, headcount, subscriber and member counts, typical price points — each read off its own page, with the source's date.

"Plans start at $49/mo, billed annually" quoted from the pricing page is the record; "mid-market SaaS pricing" destroys it. Never invent a record to fill a slot — an empty slot is an honest record.

### An observed figure is a record that one source published it
A revenue figure, a member count, or a price point is a record that this source published it on this date, never proof the category is that size or that a market exists to enter. Capture the figure in the source's words with its date; never total it into a market size or rank it. Whether it is a real market is the judged-files step's, from these records.

### Record primary surfaces, never a round-up's summary
An industry round-up or market-size blog is a place to find the primary source, never the record. Follow it to the seller's own page, the platform's own numbers, or the filing itself, and record from there.
