# Carousel

A carousel is a slide sequence on Instagram or LinkedIn. Write the copy per slide.

### Hook on the cover slide
The first slide is the hook that earns the swipe — the sharpest claim or the most useful specific, carrying the whole reason to keep going.

### One idea per slide
Each middle slide carries a single idea and stands on its own. Order the slides so each one earns the next swipe.

### Close on the last slide
The final slide carries the takeaway and the ask.
