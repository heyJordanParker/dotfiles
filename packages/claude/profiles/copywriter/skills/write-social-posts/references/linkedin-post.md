# LinkedIn Post

A LinkedIn post is a mid-length post for a work feed — a personal insight or story, never a blog-style article.
Evidence (owner, verbatim): "LI does mid-length posts (we CAN call them long posts but they're FAR shorter than articles)"

### Open on a short hook, then a short rehook
Both the hook and the rehook are short one-line things — one line on mobile — and the rehook is optional. The hook wins the tap on "see more"; the rehook holds the read past the fold.
Evidence (owner, verbatim): "LI posts have a hook & rehook and BOTH are short one-line things (one line on MOBILE) with the rehook being optional"

### End on a hand-raiser or a question
Close on a hand-raiser CTA or a question that invites a comment.
Evidence (owner, verbatim): "LI posts often end in a hand-raiser CTA or questions"

### Stack a comment beside the post when it earns one
LinkedIn sometimes stacks a comment next to its posts. A stacked comment may carry a question, a tweet, or a CTA.
Evidence (owner, verbatim): "LI also SOMETIMES stacks comments next to it's posts – a comment might have a) question b) a tweet c) a CTA"
