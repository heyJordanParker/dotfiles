# Tweet

A tweet is one self-contained short message on X. One idea, carried by a scroll-stopping first line, read in a single pass.

### Carry the whole idea in the first line
The opening line is the hook and the hook is roughly 80% of performance. Lead with the idea or the useful specific, not a wind-up. One idea per tweet.

### A tweet either is a question or has no question in it
A short tweet either IS a question or has no question in it — usually the latter. A question tacked onto the close to lift replies is engagement bait and condescends to the reader. Ask only when the whole tweet is genuinely the question.
Evidence (owner, verbatim): "a 3-line tweet is EITHER A QUESTION OR DOESN'T HAVE A QUESTION IN IT. Usually the latter. This is OBVIOUS engagement bait & it's condenscending to the audience."
