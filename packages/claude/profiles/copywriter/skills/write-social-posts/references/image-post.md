# Image Post

An image post is an image plus a caption. The copy is two pieces: the on-image text and the caption.

### Carry the hook on the image
The on-image text is the hook the feed sees first. It carries one idea and stops the scroll on its own.

### Let the caption do the rest
The caption extends the idea the image opened — the context, the specific, the ask. It reads as one thought with the image, not a restatement of it.
