# Discover Audience

Run /discover on the audience axis. Subject: the market thread. The base owns the sweep, the page gate, the trust check, the observed-facts-only rule, and the write into `research/<subject>/discovery/`. This skill adds only the audience axis below.

Inputs: the population the product serves, stated as people, from the research `Brief.md`. Excluded: owner facts, product facts, competitor records, any persona or target buyer, and any handed-in problem — the buyer and the problems are research outputs, and a guess handed in as input biases the whole thread.

## Sweep these source types

Where the people the product serves talk in the open, never on a named product's own pages. Ask: who are the sub-groups, and where do they describe their own situation? Enumerate long — the observed self-names they use, each with its citation, and the venues:

- **Forums and communities** — Reddit role and problem subreddits, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt discussion tabs, niche Slack and Discord.
- **Situation-phrase searches** — the words the people use about their own situation, surfacing the threads, blog comments, and Q&A where they describe it unprompted.
- **Job postings** — a posting is a company admitting the pain and naming the stack.

Per entry, record the buyer group it holds alongside the countable facts the base names — member or review count, post or thread activity, recency.

## Leave named-product review pages to the competitor track

A named product's review pages (G2, Capterra, app-store reviews) and its published testimonials or case studies are OUT of this thread — mining a named product means knowing the competitor, and buyer discovery stays blind to the competitor set. That work runs via research-competitor. Buyer discovery stays problem-space only.
